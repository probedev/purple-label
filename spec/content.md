### The thesis — "White label was for the old web. Purple Label is for the agent era."
For a decade, launching telehealth meant buying a no-code builder, a separate EMR, a prescribing tool, a pharmacy, and a payment processor — then wiring them together and running it all through a dozen dashboards. In 2026, your team builds with AI agents. So we skip the builders and the dashboards and give you the layer underneath: a modular, code-first platform your agents call directly. Best-of-breed modules, one API, full control. White label made you generic; **Purple Label is the tailored, AI-native version** — the same pipelines that already scaled, shaped to your brand.

### The moat — "Production infrastructure that's already passed the audits."
Anyone can generate a telehealth site. Almost no one can pass a state pharmacy-board inspection. Competitors spinning up no-code or AI-generated sites hit a wall the moment they try to ship a prescription across state lines, refund a patient, or pass a state pharmacy-board inspection. We've already paid those costs — you launch on infrastructure that's already cleared **HIPAA, pharmacy-board, and payment-processor audits**, on a HIPAA-compliant data layer with end-to-end encryption.
*Audit-cleared: HIPAA · Pharmacy-board · Payment-processor.* `[claim approved by Gregg 2026-06-25 — final wording pass with counsel before launch]`

### What you actually get *(the product form — the spine of the story)*
Three things turn into a running telemedicine business:
- **API tokens & a programmable surface.** Every module — intake, visits, prescribing, pharmacy, payments, analytics — is callable. Scoped tokens, sandbox and production.
- **Agent-ready docs & MCP servers.** Documentation formatted for AI agents to read and act on, plus MCP servers that connect your agents directly to the platform — no glue code.
- **A customizable prompt library.** Vetted prompts to build, launch, and operate each module, tailored to your brand and your agents.
*With these, your agents stand up the site, manage it, pull reporting, integrate any tool, and customize any component.* And where a human must act — a clinician reviewing a visit, an operator handling an exception — there's a focused console. Everything else is yours to assemble in code.
