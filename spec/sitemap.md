# Purple Label MD — Sitemap & Information Architecture

**Product:** Purple Label MD — a white-label telemedicine platform that lets organizations launch and operate their own fully branded virtual-care and digital-health businesses (intake → consult → e-prescribe → pharmacy fulfillment → delivery), without building the infrastructure themselves.

**Primary buyers:** DTC telehealth founders/operators, health-system & retail innovation teams, pharmacy groups, and product/clinical/IT/compliance leaders evaluating build-vs-buy.

**Conversion goals (in priority order):** (1) Request a demo / talk to sales, (2) Start free / get started, (3) Read the platform & security story deeply enough to shortlist us.

## URL & SEO conventions
- Lowercase, hyphenated, shallow (`/platform/<module>`). Trailing-slash consistent (pick one, enforce in Astro config).
- One `<h1>` per page; unique `<title>` (≤ 60 chars) and meta description (≤ 155) per page.
- Canonical tags, OpenGraph + Twitter cards per page, `sitemap.xml` + `robots.txt` (Astro integrations).
- JSON-LD: `Organization` + `SoftwareApplication` on home; `BreadcrumbList` on deep pages; `FAQPage` on Pricing.
- Internal linking: every feature page links up to `/platform` and across to `/security` + `/integrations`.
